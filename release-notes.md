YTArk 4.3

Independent sidebar controls and clean TV banner lettering.

- Search and Shorts hide independently.
- Repeated guide loads restore hidden entries without duplicates.
- The TV banner gains clean typeset lettering.

ARMv7 (armeabi-v7a) only: [APK](https://github.com/2archiver/YTArk/releases/download/v4.3.0/YTArk-v4.3.0-armeabi-v7a.apk). Not for arm64-v8a-only devices; 64-bit CPU alone is insufficient. 4K does not select an APK architecture.

Keep accounts and data; do not uninstall. If the previously installed updater cannot recover, manually install signed 4.3.0 APK over it. Package/signing identity stays unchanged. Engine: TizenTubeCobalt v2.0.3.
Notices and licenses: https://github.com/2archiver/YTArk/blob/main/NOTICE.md

Known limitations: TV permission, startup, upgrade, playback and Google TV remain unverified.
